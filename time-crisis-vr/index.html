New in 0.2.2

What's new.

  • Leaderboard: Time Crisis ranks times, like its cabinet. On the cabinet's mode screen shoot TIMED GAME, pick a stage and clear it: the game offers to publish your time on the Port0r Discord, one board per stage. The best Stage 1 time of the week of 19 October wins the hidden gun, then everyone who beats that time wins it too.
  • Since 0.2.1, your hand and arm: a gloved hand holds the gun, behind the grip and round it, the index on the trigger, the arm coming from your shoulder. Settings > Game > Arm turns it off.
  • Left-handed: Settings > Game > Gun hand. The left controller aims, shoots and vibrates, and the buttons are mirrored (the pedal on X, Start on Y).
  • Refresh rate: Settings > Smoothness > Refresh rate (72, 90 or 120 Hz). A Quest 2 runs at 72 Hz by default.
  • Log file: Settings > Game > Log file (off by default), to send us with a bug report.

Install in four steps.

About ten minutes the first time. A computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) or just the headset's browser; a USB-C cable helps.

  1. Developer modeOnce per headset: a free developer organisation on developer.oculus.com, then Meta Horizon app > Devices > Headset settings > Developer mode, and restart the headset.
  2. The appOne click from its SideQuest page, or Port0r-TimeCrisisVR-v0.2.2-quest3.apk with SideQuest or adb install -r.
  3. Your game filestimecris.zip and namcoc71.zip: the MAME 0.289 set, anywhere on the headset (Download is the simplest). The app finds them.
  4. First launchLibrary > All > Unknown sources > Port0r: Time Crisis VR. Allow "All files access": the game starts by itself.

Help

If it doesn't start.

  • Not in the Library: look under All > Unknown sources. Missing? Developer mode is off, or the headset was not restarted.
  • Files missing: check the names (timecris.zip and namcoc71.zip), that they are zips, and that "All files access" is allowed.
  • "Wrong version of the game files": your set is not for MAME 0.289. Check it with a ROM manager, put the corrected files back, return to the app.
  • Anything else: ask in #ask-port0r on the Discord, the bot answers in seconds, in your language.

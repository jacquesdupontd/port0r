Install in three steps.

  1. Developer modeOnce, from the Meta Horizon app on your phone.
  2. SideQuest or adbOne click from the game's SideQuest page, or the APK with adb install -r.
  3. Your game filesThe MAME 0.289 sets, copied anywhere on the headset: the app finds them.

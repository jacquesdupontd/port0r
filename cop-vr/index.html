New in 0.2.1

What's new.

  • Your hand and arm: a gloved hand now holds the gun, behind the grip and round it, the index on the trigger, the arm coming from your shoulder. Settings > Game > Arm turns it off.
  • The picture is no longer tilted, and the game's zooms are the cabinet's again: the whole screen can be shot at.
  • Reload with a button: A by default, any button in Settings > Buttons > Reload. Shooting off the screen still reloads.
  • Left-handed (Settings > Game > Gun hand) and two guns (Settings > Game > Two guns).
  • Settings > Game > Gun: the other gun is now a revolver, as in the game (six rounds, the cylinder in the HUD).

Install in four steps.

About ten minutes the first time. A computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) or just the headset's browser; a USB-C cable helps.

  1. Developer modeOnce per headset: a free developer organisation on developer.oculus.com, then Meta Horizon app > Devices > Headset settings > Developer mode, and restart the headset.
  2. The appOne click from its SideQuest page, or Port0r-CopVR-v0.2.1-quest3.apk with SideQuest or adb install -r.
  3. Your game filesvcop.zip and model1io2.zip: the MAME 0.289 set, anywhere on the headset (Download is the simplest). The app finds them.
  4. First launchLibrary > All > Unknown sources > Port0r: Cop VR. Allow "All files access": the game starts by itself.

Help

If it doesn't start.

  • Not in the Library: look under All > Unknown sources. Missing? Developer mode is off, or the headset was not restarted.
  • Files missing: check the names (vcop.zip and model1io2.zip), that they are zips, and that "All files access" is allowed.
  • "Wrong version of the game files": your set is not for MAME 0.289. Check it with a ROM manager, put the corrected files back, return to the app.
  • Anything else: ask in #ask-port0r on the Discord, the bot answers in seconds, in your language.

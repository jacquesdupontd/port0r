Zelda: Inside The Dream

Zelda: Inside The Dream lets you play The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (2019) in first person. You play it in VR on a Windows PC through SteamVR, or on the screen on Windows, Linux and Mac. It is free and open source.

  • You walk Koholint at Link's height, with his sword and shield in your hands.
  • You can follow Link from behind, or fly back up to the game's own view from above.
  • It is built on LAS Recomp by Peiikai, and the whole project is open.

The idea

Inside the dream.

Link's Awakening is a dream, and we have always watched it from above. Inside The Dream puts the camera in Link's eyes, so you walk the beach, the village and the dungeons at their real size.

It starts with LAS Recomp, which recompiles the game to run natively on a PC, without an emulator. This is a work in progress, so there will be bugs, and that is why the whole project is open source.

What you need

Your own copy.

Inside The Dream doesn't include the game, and there is nothing from Nintendo in the downloads. You need your own copy of Link's Awakening for Nintendo Switch, as its extracted files (the exefs and romfs folders). We play and test with version 1.0.0, and versions 1.0.1 and 1.1.0 are accepted too.

On the first launch, the app rebuilds the game's code for your own computer, once. It takes about seven minutes on a recent desktop PC, and it needs about 12 GB free.

Windows, Linux, Mac

Install it.

Each platform has its own file in the release, and the guide on GitHub goes through every step.

  1. WindowsYou run the installer, which puts an Inside The Dream shortcut on your desktop, or you unzip the zip and open lasrecomp.exe. To play in VR, you start SteamVR first, then you click PLAY IN VR.
  2. LinuxYou make the AppImage executable and open it. On the Steam Deck, you add it to Steam as a non-Steam game.
  3. MacYou drag Inside The Dream into Applications. The first time, you allow it in System Settings, Privacy & Security. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 15.
  4. First launchYou click Choose the folder and pick the folder with your exefs and romfs, then you click Set up the game.

Help

If something goes wrong.

  • A packed game file (.nsp, .xci or .nca) won't work: the app needs the extracted exefs and romfs folders.
  • If the AppImage doesn't start on Linux, run it with APPIMAGE_EXTRACT_AND_RUN=1 in front of the command.
  • On an Apple Silicon Mac with a Retina screen, we recommend setting Internal resolution to 50% in Settings > Video.
  • Anything else: ask on the Discord, in the Inside The Dream rooms.
